ANALYSIS PLAN v2 - Full paired audit: five decoding settings, illusion and game-board pairs
=========================================================================================

Written: 2026-10-26, before the full run. Replaces analysis_plan_v1.txt for everything not
yet observed. What has already been seen is stated in section 2 so the reader can tell
which tests are prospective.


1. What changed from v1, and why
--------------------------------
a. Two more decoding settings (APC-only, contrast-only), to separate VCD's two parts.
b. A second category: 20 game-board pairs (chess, sudoku, go), so the result does not rest
   on one category where LLaVA could not tell the images apart at all.
c. v1 did not say which reading wins when two apply. Fixed in section 6 (the order is the
   one the v1 analysis script already used).
d. A chance baseline for pair success (section 5), because under sampling a model that
   ignores the image still gets some pairs right by luck.
e. A minimum amount of evidence before any reading other than "inconclusive" (section 6):
   the MVP's illusion result rested on 4 changed answers.


2. What has already been seen
-----------------------------
- Illusion pairs under greedy, regular sampling and full VCD (the MVP, 840 records). The
  full run reuses these records unchanged (same run_ids, same seeds), so the illusion
  VCD-vs-regular result is already known: +0.6 points [-1.2, +2.3], 4 changed answers.
- Not yet seen: any board result, and APC-only / contrast-only on either category.
Rules c and e were written after seeing the illusion MVP result; for the boards and for
the two new settings they are prospective.


3. Data
-------
- Illusions: data/manifests/optical_pairs_v1.0.jsonl, 60 pairs from 22 templates, frozen.
- Boards: data/manifests/board_pairs_v1.0.jsonl after hand review (built as v0.1 by
  src/data/build_board_pairs.py). 20 pairs: chess 8, sudoku 8, go 4. Each board type has
  one standard image; a template is one board type with one question (rows or columns),
  so 6 templates. 10 edits add a row or column, 10 remove one. Both members of each pair
  are rendered locally with the VLMBias generator code (the released images come from a
  different generator version and have no matching standard board).
- Correct answers: illusions Yes (normal) / No (edited); boards the standard count
  (normal) / the edited count. For boards the familiar answer is the standard count, so
  on the normal image the familiar answer is the correct one.
- The board list is frozen before the run, like the illusion list.


4. Run
------
- Settings: greedy (one run per image), and regular sampling, APC-only, contrast-only and
  full VCD at seeds 42, 43, 44. Same model, prompts, temperature 1, alpha 1, beta 0.2,
  500 noise steps, per-image seeds shared by all sampling settings.
    APC-only      = official VCD sampler with alpha = 0 (filter, no contrast)
    contrast-only = official VCD sampler with beta = 0 (contrast, no filter)
- Records: (60 + 20) pairs x 2 images x 13 runs = 2080; 840 of them are the MVP records.
- The run counts only if: run_ids unique and complete; every APC-only, contrast-only and
  VCD record shows noisy-image passes and the expected first-step mask (contrast-only
  keeps all 32000 tokens, APC-only and VCD keep fewer); no greedy or regular record shows
  noisy-image passes; image checksums match the frozen manifests; the copied file matches.


5. Measures and tests
---------------------
Per category first (illusions, boards); pooled results are secondary.

Main tests (one per category, Holm across the two):
- D = mean pair score under VCD minus under regular sampling (pair score = mean pair
  success over the 3 seeds). 95% cluster-bootstrap interval and sign-flip p-value over
  templates (22 for illusions, 6 for boards), 10,000 draws, seed 0.
- With 6 board templates the sign-flip test is exact (64 sign patterns) and its smallest
  possible p-value is 1/32; the board interval will be wide. I report it anyway.

Ablation tests (one family per category, Holm-corrected within it):
- Edited-image accuracy and pair score, APC-only minus regular and contrast-only minus
  regular.

Secondary (Holm-corrected as one family per category): normal-image accuracy and
edited-image accuracy, VCD minus regular; pair score, VCD minus greedy.

Chance baseline (reported for every sampling setting):
- independent-answers pair success = mean over pairs of P(right on normal) x P(right on
  edited), each estimated from that pair's 3 seeds. Pair success above this means the
  answers on the two images are linked, i.e. the model treats them differently.
- For greedy: the share of pairs that got the same answer on both images.

Reported without tests: transition counts (fixed / broken, per image, per setting vs
regular); share of VCD's changed answers that match greedy's; per board type; adds vs
removes; for boards, error types on wrong answers (familiar count, off by one, other).


6. How the result will be read (per category)
---------------------------------------------
Order: the first matching line wins.

1. Inconclusive if: fewer than 10 answers differ between VCD and regular sampling in the
   category (on matched image and seed); or the invalid/ambiguous rate differs by more
   than 5 points between them; or D changes sign between seeds.
2. Decoding artifact if: VCD's edited-image change vs regular is nonzero and APC-only
   produces at least half of it, with the same sign, while contrast-only produces less
   than half of it; or at least half of VCD's changed answers equal greedy's answer.
3. Better grounding if: D's interval is above zero, trade-off-only cases are fewer than
   half of the edited-image fixes, and the pair score exceeds its chance baseline under
   VCD by more than under regular sampling.
4. Avoiding the familiar answer if: edited-image accuracy rises and normal-image accuracy
   falls under VCD while D's interval includes or lies below zero; or trade-off-only cases
   are at least half of the edited-image fixes.
5. Otherwise inconclusive.
An interval that includes zero is never read as "no effect"; the estimate and its bounds
are reported.


7. Robustness (11/17)
---------------------
- Drop the 12 Ebbinghaus size-mismatch pairs; drop the 4 go pairs (the generator removes
  the star points from every edited go board).
- Leave out one illusion type or board type at a time.
- Alternative parsing: score ambiguous answers by their first Yes/No or first number.
