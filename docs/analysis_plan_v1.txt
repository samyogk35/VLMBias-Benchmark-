ANALYSIS PLAN v1 - Paired audit of VCD on optical-illusion pairs
=================================================================

Written: 2026-10-27, before any paired-run results exist. The commit that adds
this file fixes its date. Nothing below is changed after results are seen; any
later change goes in a new version with the reason stated.


1. Question
-----------
When VCD changes LLaVA-1.5-7B's answers on edited illusion images, does it also
keep the normal images right? Or does it just move answers away from the
familiar one, which raises edited-image accuracy and lowers normal-image
accuracy at the same time?


2. Data
-------
- Manifest: data/manifests/optical_pairs_v1.0.jsonl (frozen in commit 819ee11).
- 60 pairs, 6 illusion types (Ebbinghaus 12, Muller-Lyer 12, Poggendorff 12,
  Zollner 12, Ponzo 6, Vertical-Horizontal 6). All 60 passed hand review.
- Every pair uses the Q1 prompt at 768 px. Correct answer: Yes on the normal
  image, No on the edited image.
- The 60 pairs come from 22 templates. Each template is one normal image shared
  by 2 or 3 pairs that differ only in how large the edit is. The 22 templates,
  not the 60 pairs, are the independent units in every test below.
- No pair is added to or removed from the main analysis after this point.


3. Run
------
- Model liuhaotian/llava-v1.5-7b at the revision in configs/paired_mvp.yaml.
- Conditions in this run:
    greedy            one run per image
    regular sampling  seeds 42, 43, 44 (temperature 1, top_p 1, no top_k)
    full VCD          seeds 42, 43, 44 (alpha 1, beta 0.2, 500 noise steps)
  Each image gets its own seed derived from the run seed, pair_id and image.
- APC-only and contrast-only are added in a later run on the same pairs and
  seeds. They feed the "decoding artifact" reading in section 6 and do not
  change the main test.
- The run counts only if all of these hold:
    * 840 records (60 pairs x 2 images x 7 runs), run_ids unique;
    * every VCD record shows at least one noisy-image pass, and no greedy or
      regular record shows any;
    * the record count still matches after copying results off the pod.


4. Scoring
----------
- Answers go through the existing parser. parse_status is valid, ambiguous or
  invalid. Ambiguous and invalid count as wrong when computing pair success,
  and their rate is reported for each condition.
- Pair success for a pair, condition and seed: both images answered correctly.
- Pair-level score per condition: mean pair success over the 3 seeds
  (0, 1/3, 2/3 or 1). Greedy has one run, so its score is 0 or 1.


5. Tests
--------
Main test (the only one that decides the result; no correction needed):
- Difference D = mean pair score under VCD minus mean pair score under regular
  sampling, over the 60 pairs.
- 95% interval: cluster bootstrap over the 22 templates, 10,000 draws, seed 0
  (cluster_bootstrap_diff).
- p-value: two-sided sign-flip test at template level, 10,000 draws, seed 0
  (cluster_signflip_test).

Secondary tests (one family, Holm-corrected):
- Normal-image accuracy, VCD minus regular.
- Edited-image accuracy, VCD minus regular.
- Pair score, VCD minus greedy.
Each uses the same bootstrap interval and sign-flip test as the main test.

Reported without tests:
- Transition counts on matched pair and seed, regular vs VCD: edited image
  fixed or broken, normal image fixed or broken, pair gained or lost, and
  "trade-off only" (edited image fixed while normal image broken in the same
  pair and seed).
- Exact McNemar on each seed separately, for normal and edited images.
- Results by illusion type and by edit size.

Sample size: with 22 independent templates the intervals will be wide.
Differences smaller than about 10 percentage points will probably come out
inconclusive. I report the estimate and interval either way, and I do not read
an interval that includes zero as "no effect".


6. How the result will be read
------------------------------
D, dC (normal-image change) and dCF (edited-image change) are all VCD minus
regular sampling.

Better grounding:
  D's interval is above zero; trade-off-only cases are fewer than half of the
  edited-image fixes; and D is positive in at least 4 of the 6 illusion types.

Avoiding the familiar answer:
  dCF > 0 and dC < 0 while D's interval includes zero or lies below it; or
  trade-off-only cases make up at least half of the edited-image fixes.

Decoding artifact (decided after the APC-only and contrast-only run):
  APC-only produces at least half of VCD's change in edited-image accuracy
  without the noisy-image contrast; or VCD's changed answers mostly match
  greedy's answers on the same images.

Inconclusive:
  none of the above fits; or the invalid/ambiguous rate differs by more than
  5 points between regular and VCD; or D changes sign between seeds.


7. Checks against misleading results
------------------------------------
- Fixed answers. For each condition, report the share of Yes answers on
  normal and on edited images. If a condition gives the same answer on 90% or
  more of all 120 images, its accuracy reflects one fixed answer rather than
  reading the images, and I say so. Before calling any VCD fix real, I read the
  raw records behind it.
- Shared images. Results are described as "60 pairs from 22 templates", never
  as 60 independent pairs.
- Robustness (scheduled for 11/17), each reported next to the main result:
    * drop the 12 Ebbinghaus pairs flagged size_mismatch (their surrounding
      circles also move);
    * leave out one illusion type at a time;
    * alternative parsing: score ambiguous answers by their first Yes/No.
- Known side changes recorded per pair in the manifest (side_changes field):
  Ebbinghaus surrounding circles move, Muller-Lyer fins and Zollner hatch
  marks move with their lines, Vertical-Horizontal figure is re-centered.


8. Expected result, stated in advance
-------------------------------------
On the full benchmark (midterm), VCD's gain over regular sampling was not
significant (p = 0.07), and the answers it changed moved toward the familiar
answer more often than away from it (59 vs 22). Mishra and D'Souza (TMLR 2026)
locate this kind of failure in what the model sees, before decoding, which a
decoding method like VCD cannot repair. I therefore expect D near zero, with
any edited-image gain offset by normal-image losses, which is the "avoiding the
familiar answer" reading. The run tests this expectation; the reading rules in
section 6 apply whatever the result is.


9. Later additions
------------------
Pairs from a second category (game boards or further illusion strengths) get
their own manifest version and are analyzed separately first. For counting
categories I also split results by whether the edit adds or removes elements,
because LLaVA-1.5 is pulled toward the familiar count differently in the two
directions (Mishra and D'Souza 2026, Table 4).
